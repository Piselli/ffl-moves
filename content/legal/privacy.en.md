# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29 September 2026** · Version 1.0

> Plain-language policy, not legal advice. We plan a lawyer review as prize pools grow.

This policy explains what personal data Form8 ("we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Controller: Form8, an independent project (legal entity to be registered). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Why | 
|---|---|
| **Email** (if you log in with email/passkey) | Login, account security, important service messages |
| **Wallet address** (Solana) | Your account identity, entries, prize claims |
| **Nickname** | Shown on leaderboards |
| **Squads, entries, scores, XP, invites** | Running the game, rankings, prizes, invite rewards |
| **Eligibility attestation** (timestamp + Terms version) | Proof you accepted the Terms and confirmed eligibility |
| **IP address, device/browser info, logs** | Security, fraud and multi-account prevention, debugging |
| **Cookies / local storage** | Keeping you logged in, language choice — see [Cookie Policy](/cookies) |

We do **not** collect your seed phrase or private keys, and we never ask for them. We do not sell your data.

## 2. Legal bases (GDPR-style)

- **Contract:** to provide the game you signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests:** security, fraud prevention, improving the service.
- **Legal obligation:** where law requires us to keep or disclose data.

## 3. Onchain data is public and permanent

Entries, payments and prize claims happen on the **Solana blockchain**. Your wallet address and these transactions are **public, visible to anyone, and cannot be changed or deleted** — by us or anyone else. Avoid linking your wallet address to your real identity if you care about privacy.

## 4. Who processes data for us

We use trusted providers who process data only to run Form8:

- **Turnkey** — embedded wallet and email/passkey authentication
- **Helius** — Solana RPC (sees wallet addresses and transactions you send)
- **Upstash** (Redis) — application data storage
- **[HOSTING PROVIDER]** — website hosting and server logs
- Public **Fantasy Premier League** data feeds — we only *read* from them; we do not send your data there.

We do **not** currently use third-party analytics.

Some providers may be located outside your country (including outside the EU/EEA). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account data (email, nickname, squads, XP): while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within **12 months** of deletion request or inactivity, unless we must keep it longer.
- Records of entries, payments and attestations: up to **5 years** for legal, tax, anti-fraud and dispute purposes.
- Server logs / IP: up to **90 days**, unless needed to investigate abuse.
- Onchain data: permanent (outside our control).

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can: **access** your data, **correct** it, **delete** it, **restrict** or **object** to processing, **port** it, and **withdraw consent**. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 7. Deleting your account

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from your account email (or tell us your wallet address and sign a message if we ask) with the subject **"Delete my data"**. We will reply within **30 days**. Note: we cannot delete blockchain records, and we may keep limited records required by law or for open disputes. Claim any unclaimed prizes before requesting deletion.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit, access control, minimal data). No system is 100% secure.

## 9. Children

Form8 is only for adults (18+, or 21+ where required, including Ukraine). We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if we learn we have, we will delete it.

## 10. Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date and notify you in the app about material changes.
