# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 29 September 2026** · Version 1.0

> Plain-language policy, not legal advice.

Cookies and similar technologies (local storage, session storage) are small pieces of data stored in your browser. Form8 keeps their use to a minimum.

## Strictly necessary (always on)

These are needed for the site to work:

- **Login session** — keeps you signed in (Turnkey embedded wallet / wallet connection).
- **Wallet adapter state** — remembers which Solana wallet you connected.
- **Language preference** — remembers EN or UA.
- **Security** — helps prevent abuse and fraud.

## Analytics

We do **not** currently use third-party analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

## No advertising cookies

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data.

## Third parties

Providers such as Turnkey, wallet extensions (Phantom, Solflare, Jupiter) and [HOSTING PROVIDER] may use their own storage needed to deliver their service. See their privacy policies.

## How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break login or other features.
