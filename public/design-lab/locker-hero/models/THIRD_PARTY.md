# Locker hero 3D assets

## iPad Pro

`ipad-pro13-m4-slim.glb` is derived from the Sketchfab model
[Ipad pro13in black m4](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ipad-pro13in-black-m4-32e1748e3b6840108ededf4359112b2f),
licensed under [CC Attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications by MoveMatch (via `@gltf-transform`): the Magic Keyboard and
Apple Pencil branches were removed (only the iPad remains, geometry untouched),
unused materials/textures were pruned, and remaining textures were re-encoded
to WebP (lossless for normal maps, q90 otherwise). 17.1 MB → 3.7 MB.
Scaled and oriented at runtime; baked screen materials are muted so the live UI
can sit in the display.
